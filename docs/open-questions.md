# Open Questions for the Owner

_Answer these when possible. Until then we use clearly marked placeholders._

## Must have (blocks launch)
- [ ] FSSAI licence number (to display on the site, as required for food businesses)
- [ ] Business address or kitchen location, and exact service area (Latur only, or which nearby towns?)
- [ ] Email address (if any) and WhatsApp number (same as 9057264895?)
- [ ] Your own photos of food, events and the kitchen (strongly preferred over stock images)
- [ ] Menus: dishes you serve for Maharashtrian, South Indian and Rajasthani
- [ ] Prices: per-plate price ranges, or whether to show "request a quote" only

## Should have
- [ ] Minimum number of guests and maximum you can serve
- [ ] How "with material" vs "without material" is priced (labour charge? per-plate?)
- [ ] Do you provide utensils, tents, decoration, serving staff, live counters?
- [ ] Advance payment and cancellation rules
- [ ] Home-food orders: which dishes, how much notice, delivery or pickup only?
- [ ] Past events or customer testimonials we may show (with their permission)
- [ ] Owner photo for the About page (only if you are happy to show it)

## Nice to have
- [ ] Preferred business logo (or permission to create a simple one)
- [ ] Preferred website address (domain name), for example bhagwaticaterers.in
- [ ] Festival or seasonal specials (Ganesh festival, Diwali, Gudi Padwa)
