# Content Plan

_All Marathi and Hindi text below is a **draft** and must be reviewed by a native speaker (the owner or family) before launch. Do not machine-translate the final site._

## Voice
Warm, respectful, simple words. Say what we do, how ordering works, and what it costs. Short sentences. No exaggerated claims.

## Core messages
1. 30+ years of experience.
2. 100% pure vegetarian.
3. Maharashtrian, South Indian and Rajasthani food.
4. For weddings, events, parties and religious functions.
5. You can give your own ingredients, or we bring everything.
6. FSSAI licensed.
7. Home-style food on order.
8. Serving Latur and nearby areas.

## Quote form fields (short)
Name, phone, event type (wedding, puja, party, corporate, home food, other), date, approximate number of guests, town, order type (we bring everything / you provide ingredients / not sure), food preference (Maharashtrian, South Indian, Rajasthani, mix; Jain, no onion/garlic), message. On submit: show thanks and open WhatsApp with the details prefilled.

## Blog and festival ideas (Phase 1b)
Wedding menu planning for Marathwada weddings; how to plan food per guest count; satyanarayan puja menu; Ganesh festival and prasad ideas; Gudi Padwa and Diwali menus; how to choose a caterer.

## Local SEO terms (starter list, see research file 02)
Marathi: लातूर केटरर्स, लातूर लग्न केटरिंग, शुद्ध शाकाहारी केटरर्स लातूर. English: catering in Latur, veg caterers Latur, wedding catering Latur. Hindi: लातूर कैटरिंग. Test each phrase in Google from Latur before finalising page titles.

## Do not
Publish prices the owner has not confirmed. Use hygiene or health superlatives. Show real customers' names or photos without permission. Use stock photos as if they were our own work.
