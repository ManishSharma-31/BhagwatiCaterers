# Bhagwati Caterers (Latur) - Visual Language, Fonts and Image Licensing

Research notes. Not legal advice. Licence summaries were read on the official pages in Nov 2026 (Unsplash, Pexels, Pixabay, unDraw, SVG Repo, Freepik/Magnific, Fontsource for fonts). Wikimedia Commons and Openverse pages could not be fetched, so those rows come from general knowledge and are marked "verify". Re-check the live terms before launch and save a dated copy of each licence page.
Nothing was downloaded. No source text is copied.

## 1. Maharashtrian visual language

### 1.1 Colour palette (colours are not copyrightable; use freely)

Notes: keep maroon/saffron/turmeric dominant; green only as a small accent (also matches the FSSAI-style veg dot). Check text contrast (WCAG AA) on cream and on maroon.

### 1.2 Motifs: can we redraw as original SVG?

General principle: copyright protects a specific artwork, not a style, a folk motif or a geometric idea. Traditional folk motifs built from basic shapes are heritage and safe to redraw in your own way. Do not trace or vectorise someone's photo or a specific artist's painting or a specific brand's design. Draw from scratch with your own geometry.

| Motif | What it is | Safe to redraw as original SVG? | Caveats |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paithani border / pallu motifs (peacock "mor", lotus, "asavali" flower creeper, parrot, coin "rui-phool") | Woven silk saree of Paithan, Maharashtra; borders in zari with bold geometric/flower and peacock designs | Yes, as an evocation: a repeating lotus + peacock border strip, drawn originally | Paithani has a GI (Geographical Indication) registration. A GI protects the name/origin for genuine product, not a ban on drawing a generic motif. Do not label graphics or goods "Paithani" as if genuine, do not copy a specific weaver's or brand's catalogue design, and avoid using the word as a product claim. Say "Paithani-inspired" in the notes only; on the site just show the style. |
| Warli art | Tribal wall art of the Warli community (Thane/Palghar); white on earthy red/brown, circles, triangles, squares, stick figures, tarpa dance circle | Yes for the visual idea: stick figures, triangle bodies, dancing circle, huts, trees. | Rangoli | Floor patterns at doorsteps (dots, lines, flowers, "sanskar bharati" style) | Yes. Prefer a plain "stitch border". |
| Banana-leaf thali (kele che pan) | Leaf with ordered mounds: salt, pickle, koshimbir, bhaji, bhat, poli, varan, sweet | Yes, fully original | Keep placement of items traditional (salt at top-left, sweet etc.). Great hero asset. |
| Kalash + shriphal (coconut) + mango leaves | Auspicious pot with coconut, betel/mango leaves | Yes, ancient, public domain heritage | Fine. |
| Toran (door garland) | Mango leaf/marigold string above doors | Yes | Fine. Marigold (zendu) strand makes a good section divider. |
| Haldi-kumkum | Turmeric and vermilion bowls (Makar Sankranti, Mangalagaur) | Yes | Fine. |
| Diya / samai / tulsi vrindavan / mandap / ghungroo / dholki / lezim / tutari / dhol-tasha | Festive icon set | Yes, draw originals | Avoid copying specific icon-set artwork (Flaticon etc.). |
| Ganesha / Vitthal / Tuljabhavani figures | Religious icons | Caution (cultural, not copyright) | A simple Ganesh symbol or "Shree" is common for caterers; ask the owner. Do not use a deity silhouette for decoration of food or footers; keep respectful. Must be redrawn by us, not taken from clipart. |
| Marathi calligraphy / "Shree" lettering | Devanagari with brush feel | Use a licensed font (below) and make lockups in SVG text-to-path | Do not use random "calligraphy" images from the web. |
| Pagadi / "Pheta" (Puneri pagdi), Maharashtrian flag shapes | Cultural headgear | Illustrated stylised icon ok | Avoid caricature, and avoid political shades (saffron + political party flags) combined on pages. Never use real people or politicians' likeness. |

Also avoid: logos or colours that resemble a government scheme, FSSAI logo (use only if the owner has a licence, and take the official artwork from FSSAI as-is), "Jay Maharashtra" slogans with political ties, and the state emblem (Emblems Act restrictions: do not use the national emblem or state government emblems on a private business site).

### 1.3 Style recommendation
Flat, bold geometric SVG with a thick maroon/turmeric line, cream background, repeated border strips (Paithani-style lotus + peacock), Warli-style white-on-maroon figure scenes as spot illustrations, and real photos of food in rounded frames. Build once as an SVG symbol sprite (borders, torans, dividers, icons) so it is a few KB and fully ours.

## 2. Typography (all on Google Fonts, SIL Open Font Licence 1.1 unless noted)

OFL 1.1 in plain words: free for commercial use, you can embed on websites, you must not sell the font itself on its own, and the font name stays with the original. No attribution is needed on the website for web use, but keep the licence text if you self-host files (best practice).

Confirm the exact names and Devanagari support on fonts.google.com before the build (use the "Marathi" language filter, or "Devanagari" script).

| Role | Family (exact name) | Scripts | Licence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Display / festive headings | **Yatra One** | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Bold, brush-like, festive; single weight. Good for hero titles. |
| Display alt | **Rozha One** | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Heavy high-contrast "wedding card" serif. Use sparingly. |
| Display fun | **Modak** | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Very rounded, playful; maybe for kid/party sections only. |
| Elegant serif for Marathi headings | **Tiro Devanagari Marathi** (+ italic) | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 (confirmed; Tiro Typeworks / The Indigo Project Authors, one weight 400 plus italic) | Calligraphic and refined, made with Marathi forms in mind; good for "invitation" feel. |
| Body, clean sans | **Noto Sans Devanagari** | Devanagari (pair with a Latin) | OFL 1.1 (confirmed; variable font, weights 100-900) | Safest wide-coverage; use for body Marathi/Hindi. |
| Body, friendly sans | **Mukta** | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Light and readable; multiple weights. Strong Marathi support. |
| Body alt | **Hind** | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Five weights; designed for UI. |
| Latin body/UI | **Poppins** | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Matches Devanagari glyphs in its own family; modern. |
| Latin serif accent | **Playfair Display** or **Cormorant Garamond** | Latin | OFL 1.1 | Optional; Latin only. |
| Handwritten accent | **Kalam** | Latin + Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Handwriting for "from the kitchen" notes. |
| Prices/menu labels | **Noto Serif Devanagari** | Devanagari | OFL 1.1 | Pairs with Noto Sans; good for menu lists. |

Recommended pairing: Headings: Yatra One (festive hero) or Tiro Devanagari Marathi (elegant). Body: Mukta (Latin and Marathi in the same family so lines match). Numbers/labels: Poppins.

Performance tips: use `font-display: swap`, subset to `latin` and `devanagari`, self-host WOFF2 (needed for good privacy in India under DPDP and for speed), max 2 families and 3-4 weights. Only load Devanagari for pages that use it, via the `unicode-range` subsets Google serves.

Attribution for fonts: not legally needed for web use; list fonts in the credits page anyway.

## 3. Copyright-safe sources

| Source | Licence in plain words | Commercial OK? | Attribution? | Risks / notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Unsplash** (free) | Free to download, copy, modify and use commercially without asking. Can't compile Unsplash photos into a competing/similar service. | Yes | Not required, appreciated | The licence does not clear people, brands or places. No model or property releases are promised, so avoid recognisable faces, signs, logos, and artworks. Unsplash+ (paid) has its own licence with releases. Few Indian catering shots; search specific. |
| **Pexels** | Free to use and modify. Not allowed: selling unaltered copies, implying that people/brands in the photo endorse you, redistributing on other stock sites, using the content as part of a trademark/business name/logo. Identifiable people must not be shown in a negative or offensive light. | Yes | Not required, appreciated | Implied endorsement is the main trap: a photo of a bride with the caterer's name can look like an endorsement. Do not put Pexels photos in the logo. Good Indian food coverage. |
| **Pixabay** | Free to use and adapt. Not allowed: selling as-is, misleading or unlawful use (especially with recognisable people), using as trademark or service mark; recognisable brands/logos cannot be used commercially in connection with goods/services. The site says consent from third parties is your responsibility. | Yes | Not required, appreciated | Pixabay does not guarantee model releases. Prefer food-only, hands-only or backs-of-heads photos. Past Pixabay content has had mislabelled uploads; save a screenshot of the licence page per image. |
| **Wikimedia Commons** (verify live page) | Each file has its own licence: public domain/CC0 (no conditions), CC BY (credit + licence link + say if changed), CC BY-SA (same plus derivative works under the same licence). Avoid NC/ND. | Yes for the types above | CC BY and BY-SA need credit; PD/CC0 do not | Check the file page for the author and licence. Personality rights, trademark and "freedom of panorama" are not solved by the licence. Lots of Indian cuisine photos by volunteers; many are CC BY-SA. For a website, a BY-SA photo used as-is is manageable (credit on the page) but avoid mixing into a composite design that would then need to be BY-SA. |
| **Openverse** (search engine, verify) | Aggregates openly licensed media from many sources (Flickr, Commons, etc.). Licence filter: choose "commercial use" and "modify". | Yes if filter used and licence checked | Per licence | Openverse only indexes; the original source licence governs and sometimes it is wrong. Click through to the original and confirm. |
| **Google Fonts / Material Symbols** | Fonts under OFL; Material Symbols/Icons under Apache 2.0 | Yes | Apache 2.0 requires keeping licence notice if redistributing the files; list in credits | Icons are generic UI icons (phone, WhatsApp-style glyphs not included). WhatsApp, Instagram, Google logos: trademarks, use the brands' official asset guidelines. |
| **unDraw** | Free for personal and commercial use, modify freely, no credit needed. Not allowed: bundling/redistributing, scraping, AI training, using brand logos beyond representing the brand | Yes | No | Style is western flat-startup and does not look Maharashtrian; can recolour to our palette but it will not feel local. Use sparingly or not at all. |
| **SVG Repo** | A distributor. Default "SVG Repo licence" allows commercial use and adaptation without credit but not reselling as-is; but each icon can be Public Domain/CC0, MIT/Apache/OFL, CC BY, BY-SA, BY-NC (not OK), or Logo (trademark). | Only if the item's own licence allows | Depends per item | Must check the licence line on every asset page. | **Freepik** (now Magnific branding on some docs) | Free tier requires credit to the author/Freepik in most uses; premium plans generally do not. Rules on templates, packaging, products with the content as main element. Cannot share editable files with multiple clients. | Yes, with attribution (free) | Free: yes | Credit rules are fussy, and rights are not transferable. Avoid for a small site unless the owner buys a plan. Never copy "Indian wedding" vector packs into our own art. |
| **Flaticon / Vecteezy / Canva stock** | Mixed, usually attribution on free tier | Check | Often | Skip unless needed. |
| **AI-generated art** | Copyright status unclear in India; generator terms vary | Risky | n/a | Not recommended for a brand with identifiable culture; models may reproduce real people or copyrighted designs. |
| **Government / PIB / Maharashtra Tourism images** | Often "all rights reserved" or "Government Open Data" with conditions | Check | Check | Do not use unless the licence says so. |

Common rules for ALL photos:
1. A licence on the photo covers copyright only. It does not cover privacy/publicity rights of recognisable people, trademarks/logos on packaging, temples or artworks in the photo.
2. Prefer photos without faces. If faces are in frame, only use if the site states a model release (Unsplash+, paid stock) or if it's the owner's own event with signed consent.
3. Blur or crop visible brands (soft-drink bottles, sauce sachets, branded steel counters).
4. Never use a photo of a real public figure (politicians, actors, cricketers) or a celebrity wedding, even if the photo is licensed.
5. Don't present stock photos as "our food" or "our events": under Indian consumer law (CCPA misleading-advertisement guidance) this is a misrepresentation risk. Label them "representative image" in small print or avoid.
6. Dishes: stock photos often show non-Maharashtrian versions (e.g. a Delhi-style thali labelled "Maharashtrian"). Check authenticity (puran poli has a ghee-soaked yellow-brown stuffed poli; a modak has the pleated top).

### 3.1 Owner-supplied photos (best option) - workflow

Why: authentic, the right dishes, zero licence risk, and builds trust.

1. Shoot list (about 40 shots, 2 hours with a modern phone): see section 4. Brief the owner: daylight, clean steel/plate, banana leaf, plain wooden/cloth background, no hands wearing branded items, no customers' faces.
2. Phone settings: highest-quality JPEG/HEIC, landscape for hero (16:9), square for menu items, hold steady. Wipe the lens.
3. Send via WhatsApp "Document" mode (not as a photo) or Google Drive to keep full quality (WhatsApp compresses).
4. Consent: for any photo with people, get a short written/WhatsApp consent from each person (name + "OK to use on website"). Keep it in a folder `consents/`. Staff are to sign a standard release.
5. Owner ownership: the person who presses the shutter owns the copyright. If a hired photographer or a relative takes the photos, get a one-line written assignment/licence ("I allow Bhagwati Caterers to use these photos on its website and social media without time limit").
6. Naming: `puran-poli-01.jpg`, with a spreadsheet of caption + alt text (Marathi and English).
7. Until photos arrive: build with original SVG illustrations (banana-leaf thali, modak, kalash, Warli figures) and neutral CSS-gradient placeholders with the Marathi dish name. Each placeholder `<img>` has a `data-credit="placeholder"` flag so none ships live without an approved image. Swap in the owner's photos with identical filenames and aspect ratios.
8. Stock fallback only for abstract shots (flowers, marigold, diya, empty mandap decor) and for dishes where the owner has no photo. Log every one (section 5.3).

## 5. Image optimisation and credits

### 5.1 Performance (mobile first; many Latur users on 4G, mid-range Android)

| Topic | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Formats | Serve AVIF with WebP and JPEG fallbacks via `<picture>`. SVG for all illustrations/icons. PNG only where transparency is needed and SVG not possible. |
| Sizes (hero) | Generate 480, 768, 1200, 1600 px widths; use `srcset` + `sizes`. Hero at 1600 px AVIF about 80-150 KB, WebP 120-200 KB target. |
| Sizes (menu cards) | 400 px square (+800 for retina); about 20-40 KB each. |
| Quality | AVIF q 45-55, WebP q 70-78, JPEG q 75 mozjpeg. Strip EXIF (removes GPS, camera info; keeps privacy). |
| Loading | `loading="lazy"` and `decoding="async"` for everything below the fold. Hero: no lazy, `fetchpriority="high"`, preload the LCP image. Always set `width` and `height` to avoid layout shift. |
| Placeholders | Tiny blurred base64 or dominant colour (LQIP) behind each image. |
| SVG | Run through SVGO; inline small sprites; target < 20 KB for the border sprite; avoid raster embedded inside SVG. |
| Fonts | WOFF2, subset, 2 families max, `font-display: swap`. |
| Tooling | `sharp` or `squoosh-cli` / `cwebp` / `avifenc` in a build script; or an image CDN (Cloudflare, Cloudinary free tier) later. |
| Budgets | Home page below 1.0 MB on first view, LCP under 2.5 s on slow 4G, CLS under 0.1 (check with Lighthouse). |
| Alt text | Marathi and English alt for every image (accessible and good SEO for "Latur catering"). |

### 5.2 Credits page
- Make a `/credits` page ("Photo and design credits"), linked in the footer in small text.
- Content: photographers (name, source link, licence) for any stock or CC image; fonts list with OFL; icon sets; "Illustrations by [studio], inspired by Maharashtrian folk art".
- CC BY / BY-SA items must show author, title, licence name with link, and "changed" if edited, placed near the image or on the credits page where the image is clearly tied (with a caption link "Credits").
- Public domain/CC0/Unsplash/Pexels/Pixabay: not legally required, but credit is courteous and costs nothing.

### 5.3 Provenance log (one CSV, kept in the repo)
Columns: `file`, `used_on_page`, `type (own/stock/svg)`, `source_url`, `author`, `licence`, `licence_url`, `date_checked`, `screenshot_path`, `people_visible (Y/N)`, `release_on_file`, `brand_logos_visible (Y/N)`, `attribution_text`, `approved_by`.
Rules: no image merges without a filled row; re-verify licences before launch and yearly; keep a screenshot/PDF of the licence page on the download date (licences change, e.g. Unsplash changed its terms in 2017 and added Unsplash+ later).

## 6. Recommended decisions

1. **Photos:** owner's own photos for all food and event images (shoot list from section 4); stock only for abstract/atmospheric shots, food-only, no faces.
2. **Graphics:** draw an original SVG kit (Paithani-style border, Warli scene, kalash, toran, rangoli, thali, modak, diya, badges) in the maroon/saffron/turmeric/leaf-green palette. Avoid unDraw/Freepik-style Western art.
3. **Stock sources order:** Pexels / Unsplash (checking people and logos), then Pixabay, then Wikimedia Commons (only CC0/PD/CC BY, avoid BY-SA unless credited), Openverse to find, then confirm at the source. Skip Freepik free tier and SVG Repo except for items with a clearly CC0/PD licence.
4. **Fonts:** Yatra One (festive headings), Tiro Devanagari Marathi (elegant Marathi), Mukta (body), Poppins (UI numbers); all OFL, self-hosted WOFF2.
5. **Cultural caution:** "inspired by" wording for Paithani and Warli (GI crafts), no deity icons without owner OK, no state emblem, no politicians or public figures, no swastika glyphs.
6. **People:** no recognisable faces unless a signed consent exists; blur logos; label stock as "representative image" or avoid.
7. **Process:** provenance CSV and a `/credits` page from day one; placeholders flagged until real images arrive.
8. **Performance:** AVIF/WebP via `<picture>`, lazy loading, width/height on all images, hero under about 150 KB, page under about 1 MB.
9. **Open items for the owner:** logo status (existing logo or new), religious icon preference, FSSAI number display, whether a photo session can happen in the next 2 weeks.
10. **Verify before launch:** the Wikimedia Commons and Openverse terms, the exact Google Fonts family list for Devanagari, and the GI details, since those pages could not be fetched in this session.
